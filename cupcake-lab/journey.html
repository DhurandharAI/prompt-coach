Cupcake Lab PH — Build Plan Prepared 25 July 2026

From an Instagram post to a paid order, without the DM backlog.

A WooCommerce storefront on Hostinger, PayMongo taking cards, GCash and bank transfers, and every confirmed order fanned out to the Telegram channels the team already lives in — for roughly ₱350 a month in fixed cost.

The customer journey

Seven stages. The first four are the customer alone on their phone; the last three are where your team takes over.

01
She sees the postInstagram / Facebook

Every post, reel and story carries either a tagged product (synced automatically from the website's catalogue to Meta) or a link sticker. The link is tagged so you can tell, later, which post sold what — for example cupcakelab.ph/product/red-velvet-box?utm_source=ig&utm_campaign=jul-launch. The link in bio points at a page on your own site rather than Linktree.

02
She lands on the exact productWebsite

Not the homepage — the cupcake she just saw, with the same photograph. She picks flavours, box size, dedication text, and for a custom design uploads a reference photo or chooses from the approved-designs gallery. Then delivery or pickup, with the lead-time rules enforced for her (no orders for tomorrow when you need two days).

03
She checks outWebsite

One page: name, mobile, Viber number, email, address and delivery date. The Viber number is asked for explicitly and framed as "where we'll set up your order group" — so it arrives correct instead of being chased for later.

04
She paysPayMongo

Card with 3D Secure, GCash, Maya, GrabPay, or bank transfer through BPI and UnionBank online banking and QRPh. Payment either succeeds or it doesn't — no screenshots to verify, no "sent na po" to reconcile by hand.

05
The order lands with your teamTelegram MCJC

The moment PayMongo confirms payment, an order card appears in #orders with her Viber number ready to copy, the money lands in #payables with the fee and net already worked out, and a custom design request goes to #design with her reference image attached.

06
Her Viber group opensViber

Whoever is on duty creates the group from the order card — named to a convention like CL #1042 – Ana R. – Aug 2 — adds her and the production lead, and pastes the welcome template. Her confirmation page has already told her to expect the invite within two business hours, so the wait is expected rather than worrying.

07
It gets baked and deliveredTelegram → Viber

At 6am #production gets the day's bake list with flavours already totalled, and #dispatch gets the delivery manifest with addresses and time windows. Design approvals, delivery updates and the finished photo all reach her in the Viber group — one thread per order, nothing lost in the main inbox.

One thing worth knowing up front

Viber groups can't be created by software

Viber's API does not allow any app to create a group chat or add members to one. That part stays manual on every platform, ours included — so the plan makes it fast rather than pretending to automate it: the order card arrives with the number, the group name, and the welcome message ready to paste, and the customer has already been told to expect the invite within two business hours.

If you later want it fully hands-off, the alternative is a Viber bot that opens a one-to-one thread when she taps a link on the thank-you page. That works without anyone lifting a finger, but it's a private chat rather than a group — which is a change to how you work today, so I'd start with the group.

Four weeks to live

Week 1 — Foundation

Hosting and domain set up, WordPress and WooCommerce installed, a staging site alongside it, and your designer's mockup rebuilt as the real theme with the actual products and photographs.

Week 2 — Commerce

Flavours, box sizes and dedications as real product options. Delivery zones, fees and lead times. PayMongo tested end to end in test mode, then switched to live keys.

Week 3 — Ops wiring

The payment webhook, the Telegram bot across all eight channels, the Slack connection, the daily digests, and the Viber group procedure with its message templates.

Week 4 — Social bridge and launch

Catalogue synced to Meta so posts can be tagged, link-in-bio page, tracking on every link, analytics, real test orders placed by the team, then live.
