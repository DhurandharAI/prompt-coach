A visual walkthrough of the WooCommerce theme rebuilt from your designer's mockup, plus the checkout and the ops notification your team will actually work from.

Everything below uses the real design tokens from theme/cupcakelab/style.css, the real prices from the cleaned catalogue, and the real product photos pulled off the mockup.

⚠️

This is a static picture, not a working shop. Nothing here is clickable — a WordPress theme needs WordPress, MySQL and WooCommerce running behind it, and the site has not been deployed to Hostinger yet. Treat this as a design proof: it tells you whether the look and the flow are right before anyone pays for hosting.

Panel 6 — ops notification

What lands in Telegram the moment payment clears

Posted to #orders by the webhook handler. Viber's API cannot create group chats, so the card does the next best thing: it pre-formats the group name to your convention and makes both it and the customer's number tap-to-copy, then posts the welcome message separately so it can be pasted straight in.

Note the dedication reading Mum & Dad. An unescaped ampersand makes Telegram reject the whole message, which would silently lose the order card — there is a test covering exactly that case.
